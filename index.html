The Status Quo: Fixed-time traffic signals operate on outdated schedules, infrastructure is aging and deteriorating, modernization requires massive capital expenditure, and implementation timelines stretch across decades.

Why Retrofit?

A faster, more affordable path to intelligent traffic systems.

Traditional Smart City Approach

  • Replace infrastructure entirely
  • High upfront capital costs
  • Long implementation timelines
  • Complex procurement processes
  • High execution risk
  • Requires sustained funding

TRACFRICA Retrofit Approach

  • Upgrade existing intersections
  • Lower deployment costs (70% reduction)
  • Faster time to impact (6-12 months)
  • Incremental, modular scaling
  • Proven edge technology stack
  • Immediate operational benefits

How the System Works

A five-step adaptive traffic control pipeline.

1
Cameras Capture
IP cameras monitor traffic flow at intersections
2
Edge Processing
Lightweight edge devices process data locally
3
AI Detection
YOLOv8 detects and classifies vehicles in real-time
4
Adaptive Optimization
Algorithms compute optimal green-time allocation
5
Central Monitoring
Traffic Control Center monitors the entire network
